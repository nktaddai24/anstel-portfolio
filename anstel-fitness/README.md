# ANSTEL FITNESS
### Build Muscle. Move Better. Train for Life.

**A Human Garage**

## 1. Project Overview

ANSTEL FITNESS is a fitness and wellness brand focused on strength development, mobility, physical performance and long-term well-being.

The brand combines structured exercise, responsible training, movement education and personalised coaching to help beginners, everyday adults and experienced fitness enthusiasts develop sustainable fitness habits.

This repository contains the development of the official ANSTEL FITNESS website.

## 2. Brand Identity

- **Business name:** ANSTEL FITNESS
- **Brand concept:** A Human Garage
- **Tagline:** Build Muscle. Move Better. Train for Life.
- **Primary colours:** Black and white
- **Logo:** Official ANSTEL logo supplied by the founder.

## 3. Mission

To empower beginners and fitness enthusiasts to build strength, improve mobility and develop sustainable training habits through proper preparation, controlled movement, education and purposeful coaching.

## 4. Vision

To become a recognised human-performance and movement-development brand, empowering people to build strength, improve mobility and maintain their physical capabilities throughout life.

## 5. Our Story

ANSTEL FITNESS was established to provide fitness enthusiasts and beginners with a more considered approach to physical development.

The founder's personal experiences with shoulder and hip injuries highlighted the importance of proper preparation, controlled movement and responsible training progression.

This experience inspired the Human Garage concept: a place where people can develop their physical capabilities through structured exercise, mobility work and purposeful coaching.

ANSTEL FITNESS aims to make fitness more accessible, educational and sustainable.

## 6. Training Philosophy

Our approach is built around four principles:

1. **Preparation:** Preparing the body appropriately before physical activity.
2. **Controlled Movement:** Performing exercises with control and responsible progression.
3. **Physical Development:** Building strength, mobility and functional movement.
4. **Long-term Well-being:** Prioritising sustainable physical development over temporary results.

## 7. Target Audience

ANSTEL FITNESS serves:

- Beginners starting their fitness journey.
- Everyday adults seeking to improve their physical fitness.
- Experienced gym enthusiasts looking for structured training.

## 8. Services

ANSTEL FITNESS offers ten core services:

2. Personal Training

6. Muscle-Building Programmes

## 9. Delivery Model

ANSTEL FITNESS operates through three delivery channels:

- **Physical gym:** Training at the ANSTEL FITNESS facility.
- **Online coaching:** Remote training guidance and programme support.
- **Preferred locations:** Training delivered at suitable locations selected by clients.
